Follow these steps on the live demos and evaluation—fictional data only.

  1. Open the repair desk demo on the main site. Leave mode on Saved AI results.
  2. Pick a holdout scenario (e.g. one flagged for technician review) and click Run the demo. Read the judgment trace and final outcome.
  3. Open the same case on the evaluation page. Filter Baseline mismatches to see where keywords fail and the workflow does not.
  4. Switch to Parts & listings. Change cost assumptions and confirm margins update to the cent.
  5. Skim desktop/Linux tools on the résumé for non-AI engineering depth.
